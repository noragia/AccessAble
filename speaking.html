Topics

The perspectives I offer.

Speaking topics
TopicThe ideaYou'll leave knowing how to
Advocating for YourselfKeynote or workshop Most people don't have a confidence problem. They have an asking problem. They hint instead of ask, wait to be noticed, and apologize before they've said anything.
  • Ask for what you want without over-explaining it
  • Answer when someone underestimates you
  • Stand behind your idea when the room pushes back
Selling YourselfKeynote or workshop Everyone's selling something: an idea, a raise, themselves in an interview. Most people wing it. I spent years in tech sales learning what actually gets a yes, and it works on a lot more than software.
  • Figure out what the other person actually needs before you pitch
  • Match what you bring to that need, in plain words
  • Ask for the yes without feeling gross about it
ResilienceKeynote or workshop Things go wrong. Sometimes very wrong. I lost everything in an apartment fire my senior year of college. You don't get to skip the hard part, but you do get to decide what it turns into.
  • Reframe a setback without pretending it's fine
  • Sort what's in your control from what isn't
  • Take the next step when you'd rather quit
