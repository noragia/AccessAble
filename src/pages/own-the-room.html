Let's be honest for a second. 👀

I used to be the person who listened to motivational podcasts, read self-help books, and spent way too much time scrolling Instagram watching other people live the life I wanted.

I'd tell myself I was getting inspired.

I was learning.

I was preparing.

But when was I actually going to DO something?

Because you can spend hours consuming content about building a business, getting your dream job, or becoming a better speaker…

and still be in the exact same place six months later.

I've also heard women say things like:

  • “I don't want to join that club. It's too male-dominated.”
  • “I could never get up and speak in front of people.”
  • “I'm just not good at selling myself.”
  • “I'm not qualified enough to apply for that.”
  • “I have an idea, but I wouldn't even know how to pitch it.”
  • “Maybe I'll do it next year.”

And I'm going to be blunt with you.

  • You don't have to be the best public speaker in the room.
  • You don't need every qualification.
  • You don't need everyone to approve of what you're doing.

But you DO need to learn how to put yourself forward, communicate what you bring to the table, and actually take action.

Because nobody else can do that part for you.

Imagine being able to…

  • Walk into an interview and confidently say why you're the right person for the job.
  • Pitch your business or idea without questioning every word.
  • Give a presentation without worrying what everyone thinks.
  • Ask for the promotion, opportunity, or introduction you've been wanting.
  • Sell yourself without feeling uncomfortable about it.
  • Handle rejection without letting it convince you to give up.

And the best part? You don't need to wait until you feel ready to start.
