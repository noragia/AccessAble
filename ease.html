The Doors

The best hotels earn their Doors.

Scores come from guests. Doors come from AccessAble, after an in-person walkthrough.

One Door

Easy to navigate. Guests can get around the whole hotel, accommodations are there when needed, and most things are within reach.

Two Doors

Above and beyond. Standout amenities and bathrooms, and accessible options beyond the standard room.

Three Doors

Exceptional everywhere. Service that goes above and beyond, and accessible options in every room type, suites included. Very few hotels will earn this.

  • 50+reviews before a hotel can be considered
  • 80 / 90 / 95Access Score needed for One, Two or Three Doors
  • 3 yearsDoors are renewed every three years
  • $0Hotels can never pay for a score or for Doors
