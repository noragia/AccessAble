The work

Three skills.

Everything I teach, on stage or in a workshop, comes back to these. Here's what each one actually means.

01

Advocating for Yourself

Most people don't have a confidence problem. They have an asking problem. They hint instead of ask, wait to be noticed, and apologize before they've said anything.

You'll leave knowing how to
  • Ask for what you want without over-explaining it
  • Answer when someone underestimates you
  • Stand behind your idea when the room pushes back
02

Selling Yourself

Everyone's selling something: an idea, a raise, themselves in an interview. Most people wing it. I spent years in tech sales learning what actually gets a yes, and it works on a lot more than software.

You'll leave knowing how to
  • Figure out what the other person actually needs before you pitch
  • Match what you bring to that need, in plain words
  • Ask for the yes without feeling gross about it
03

Resilience

Things go wrong. Sometimes very wrong. I lost everything in an apartment fire my senior year of college. You don't get to skip the hard part, but you do get to decide what it turns into.

You'll leave knowing how to
  • Reframe a setback without pretending it's fine
  • Sort what's in your control from what isn't
  • Take the next step when you'd rather quit
