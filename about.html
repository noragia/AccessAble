About

Was told I couldn't. Did it anyway.

I've learned a thing or two about being underestimated, putting yourself out there, and figuring things out along the way. Now I help others do the same.

The short version

A few times I asked anyway.

  • College

    Walked onto my college rowing team as a coxswain. Zero experience.

  • Sales club

    Became co-president of my college sales club, competed in sales competitions, then started running them.

  • Senior year

    Lost my home in an apartment fire. Rebuilt from scratch.

  • Graduation

    Gave the student commencement address at the Isenberg School of Management, Class of 2026.

  • Now

    Left tech sales to build AccessAble.

Why AccessAble

I'm building the thing I wish someone had handed me.

Nobody teaches you how to ask for the raise, pitch yourself in an interview, or get back up after something knocks you flat. You figure it out the hard way, or you don't.

I want to be the role model I never had growing up.
