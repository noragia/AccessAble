Perspectives

Speaking topics
TopicThe ideaTakeaways
Take Up SpaceKeynote Everyone has been the one who doesn't fit in a room. Most people get quieter. I didn't. This talk is about what happens when you stop hiding the thing that makes you stand out.
  • Speak up when you'd rather stay quiet
  • Own what makes you different instead of hiding it
  • Move before you feel ready
Why Not Me?Keynote People told me I couldn't my whole life. I went into tech sales anyway, co-led my college sales club, and started my own business. This talk is about going after what you want before anyone gives you permission.
  • Get clear on what you actually want
  • Ask for it in plain words
  • Keep going after the first no
ResilienceKeynote Things go wrong. Sometimes very wrong. I lost everything in an apartment fire my senior year of college. You don't get to skip the hard part, but you do get to decide what it turns into.
  • Reframe a setback without pretending it's fine
  • Sort what's in your control from what isn't
  • Take the next step when you'd rather quit
